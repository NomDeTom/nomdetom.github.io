    What it removes

    • Amazon — reduced to /dp/ASIN. Drops the product-name slug, ref=, tag= (affiliate), search keywords and everything else.
    • AliExpress — reduced to /item/ID.html. Drops spm, algo_pvid, pdp_npi and the rest of the query.
    • eBay — reduced to /itm/ID.
    • YouTube — keeps v, t and list; drops si, feature, pp.
    • Google search — keeps only q.
    • Everywhere — utm_*, fbclid, gclid, msclkid, mc_cid/mc_eid, igshid, ttclid, twclid, yclid, _ga, _gl, _hsenc/_hsmi, ref_src, srsltid, spm, scm and similar.
    • Short links (a.aliexpress.com/_…, amzn.to, s.click.aliexpress.com) redirect through a tracker and cannot be cleaned without following them; they are flagged, not changed.
