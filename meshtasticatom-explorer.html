What this is. Output of the SF++ set-reconciliation simulator in NomDeTom/Meshtasticatom, built from the run digests on its sim-results branch by a scheduled job. Nothing on this page is hand-written or hand-edited.

What it is not. Not a measurement of a real network. The radio model, terrain and clutter come from Meshtasticator and Komzpa's terrain stack; the mesh, traffic and archive behaviour are simulated. held and union are what an archive holds, not what a user receives.

Reading it. The four delivery measures have four different denominators and are not comparable to each other. demand is aggregate airtime demand across the whole mesh, a multiple with no ceiling; chutil is what a device reports and cannot exceed 100%.

Licence. This page is output of the simulator in NomDeTom/Meshtasticatom, which is distributed under GPL-3.0 because parts of it are transcribed from Meshtastic firmware. A program's output is not the program: this page describes a simulation and is free to read and quote. The simulator's Meshtasticator half remains CC BY 4.0, attribution preserved. Part of that simulator's source is based on Spinsante, Gioacchini and Scalise, A novel experimental-based tool for the design of LoRa networks (MetroInd4.0&IoT, 2019), which stems from Bor, Roedig, Voigt and Alonso, Do LoRa Low-Power Wide-Area Networks Scale? (MSWiM '16) and the LoRaSim library. Terrain, clutter and the capture-aware physics are from Komzpa's Meshtasticator branch.
