What this is. Output of the SF++ set-reconciliation simulator in NomDeTom/Meshtasticatom, built from the run digests on its sim-results branch by a scheduled job. Nothing on this page is hand-written or hand-edited.

What it is not. Not a measurement of a real network. The radio model, terrain and clutter come from Meshtasticator and Komzpa's terrain stack; the mesh, traffic and archive behaviour are simulated. held and union are what an archive holds, not what a user receives.

Reading it. The four delivery measures have four different denominators and are not comparable to each other. demand is aggregate airtime demand across the whole mesh, a multiple with no ceiling; chutil is what a device reports and cannot exceed 100%.
